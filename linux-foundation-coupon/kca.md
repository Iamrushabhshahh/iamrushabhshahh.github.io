## Frequently asked questions

**What is the KCA certification?**

The Kyverno Certified Associate, a 90-minute multiple-choice exam on Kyverno fundamentals, installation, the CLI, and writing, applying and managing policies.

**How much is the KCA with a discount code?**

List price is $250. With RUSHABH30 it drops to about $175, a saving of roughly $75.

**Is KCA a security certification?**

Partly. It covers policy enforcement, which is a security control, but it is narrower than the CKS. If you want a broad Kubernetes security credential, CKS is the one; KCA proves you can enforce policy with a specific tool.

**Kyverno or OPA?**

Kyverno expresses policy as Kubernetes resources, OPA uses Rego, a separate language. Neither is objectively better, but Kyverno has the gentler learning curve for people who already think in YAML manifests. This exam only covers Kyverno.
