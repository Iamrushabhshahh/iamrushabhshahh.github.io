## Why it's worth it

Platform engineering suffers from being a job title before it was a defined discipline, and this exam is the Linux Foundation writing down what it thinks the discipline is. Over a third of it is core fundamentals, which is unusually top-heavy and tells you it is aimed at people who have not done the job yet. Useful as an orientation and as the step below CNPE.

## Prep tips

- Pay attention to the measurement domain. It is only 8% but it is the part most practitioners have never thought about, and the one that separates a platform from a pile of tooling.
- You do not need to have built an internal developer platform, but you should have used one and be able to say what made it good or bad.
- Multiple choice and 120 minutes, so it is a recall exam. Read the curriculum and map each item to something you have seen rather than practising in a terminal.

## Frequently asked questions

**What is the CNPA certification?**

The Certified Cloud Native Platform Engineering Associate, a 120-minute multiple-choice exam covering platform engineering fundamentals, observability, delivery, platform APIs and developer experience.

**How much is the CNPA with a discount code?**

List price is $250. With RUSHABH30 it drops to about $175, a saving of roughly $75.

**CNPA or CNPE, which should I take?**

CNPA if you are moving into platform work and want the map. CNPE if you already do it and want a credential that proves you can build. CNPA is the cheaper, multiple-choice orientation; CNPE is performance-based at $445.

**Are there prerequisites?**

None formally. In practice you will struggle without hands-on Kubernetes experience, since almost every domain assumes a cluster underneath.
