## Frequently asked questions

**What is the CNPE certification?**

The Certified Cloud Native Platform Engineer, a performance-based Linux Foundation exam covering platform architecture, GitOps delivery, self-service APIs, observability and policy enforcement. Two hours in a live terminal.

**How much is the CNPE with a discount code?**

List price is $445. With RUSHABH30 it drops to about $311, a saving of roughly $134.

**Is the CNPE harder than the CKA?**

Different rather than harder. The CKA tests whether you can operate a cluster; the CNPE tests whether you can design something other engineers build on top of. It assumes CKA-level knowledge as a starting point.

**Should this be my first certification?**

No. It is aimed at people already doing platform work. If you are new to Kubernetes, the CKA is the better purchase and the natural prerequisite in practice, even though it is not a formal one.

**What is the difference between CNPE and CNPA?**

CNPA is the associate-level, multiple-choice version covering the same territory at a recall level. CNPE is performance-based and costs more because it asks you to actually build. CNPA is the sensible stepping stone.
