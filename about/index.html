About Rushabh Shah

Rushabh Shah is a DevOps engineer at Orocorp Technologies Private Limited in Ahmedabad, India. He is a Docker Captain and a Grafana Champion, both selected in 2026, in the community programmes run by Docker and Grafana Labs. His day job is Kubernetes in production: Helm and Argo CD for delivery, Terraform for infrastructure, and the Grafana LGTM stack with OpenTelemetry for observability. He has cut cloud spend by 59 percent and led a zero downtime cloud migration. He organizes Grafana & Friends Ahmedabad, translated ebpf.io into Hindi, and has Hindi translations of the Kubernetes docs in review.
