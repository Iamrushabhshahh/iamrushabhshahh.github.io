**How much is FinOps Certified: AI Value with a discount code?**

**How is FinOps for AI different from regular cloud FinOps?**

The levers change. There is no instance to rightsize on token-priced inference, GPU capacity is committed to rather than scaled on demand, and cost per unit of business value is much harder to attribute. The framework still applies; the specific optimization and allocation techniques do not transfer directly.

**Is this certification worth it if we only use hosted APIs?**

Yes, arguably more so. API-billed AI spend is the easiest kind to lose track of, because it needs no provisioning and shows up as one line on a bill that nobody owns.
